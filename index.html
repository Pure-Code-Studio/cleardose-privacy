Privacy Policy for ClearDose

Last updated: November 27, 2026

ClearDose ("the app") is published by Ash & Crown ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles.

Medication data

Medication names, doses, schedules, notes, and history are stored only on your device. They are never sent to Ash & Crown, and they are never sent to or shared with ad networks. There is no account, no login, and no cloud sync.

Advertising

The free version shows Google AdMob banner ads. Those ads are non-personalized only. There are no full-screen or rewarded ads.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK, provided by Google, collects the device's advertising ID and device and diagnostic information, and uses the IP address to estimate approximate (not precise) location, for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use their services.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

In regions that require consent, the app may show Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form before an ad is requested.

Premium

Premium is a $4.99 one-time purchase through Google Play. It removes ads, and the Google Mobile Ads SDK is not started for Premium users. Google Play handles the purchase, and Ash & Crown does not receive payment card details.

Permissions

  • Notifications and exact alarms — to remind you at the times you set.
  • Run at device startup (boot) — to re-arm your local reminders after your phone restarts.
  • Internet, network state, and Advertising ID (AD_ID) — used by the free version to show AdMob banner ads. The Mobile Ads SDK is not started when Premium is unlocked.
  • Advertising services (ad ID, attribution, and topics) — declared by the Google Mobile Ads SDK for the free version's banner.
  • Google Play Billing — to complete the one-time Premium purchase.
  • Foreground service — included by a bundled background-work library. ClearDose does not start its own foreground service.
  • App-only signature permission — lets this app's own components talk to each other. Other apps cannot use it.

Vibration, wake lock, and full-screen alerts are also used so reminders can reach you at the scheduled time.

Security and deletion

Network traffic for ads and purchases is encrypted in transit. You can delete your medication data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. Uninstalling permanently deletes medication and history data from your device. Ash & Crown does not keep a server-side copy.

Children's privacy

ClearDose is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Medical disclaimer

ClearDose is a reminder and personal record-keeping tool only. It does not provide medical advice, does not recommend dosages, and does not check for drug interactions. Always follow the instructions given by your doctor or pharmacist.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The "Last updated" date at the top will change when we do.
