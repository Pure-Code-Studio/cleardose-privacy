Privacy Policy for ClearDose

Last updated: October 21, 2026

ClearDose ("the app") is developed by Ash & Crown ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we collect

ClearDose does not collect, transmit, or store any personal or health data on any server. All medication names, dosage notes, schedules, and adherence history you enter are stored only on your device, in a local database. We never see it, and it is never sent anywhere. There is no account, no login, and no cloud sync.

Third-party services

  • Google Play Billing processes the optional one-time Premium purchase. Payment information is handled entirely by Google; we never receive your payment details.

ClearDose does not display advertising and does not use any ad network.

Permissions the app requests

  • Notifications: to remind you when it's time to take a medication.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarm scheduling): so reminders fire at the precise time you set, not an approximate window.
  • Run at device startup (boot): solely to re-arm your local medication reminders after your phone restarts. Nothing is transmitted.

All of the above are used purely to run reminders on your own device.

Medical disclaimer

ClearDose is a reminder and personal record-keeping tool only. It does not provide medical advice, does not recommend dosages, and does not check for drug interactions. Always follow the instructions given by your doctor or pharmacist.

Data deletion

Because all data is local, uninstalling the app permanently deletes all medication and history data. There is no server-side copy to separately request deletion of.

Children's privacy

ClearDose is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
