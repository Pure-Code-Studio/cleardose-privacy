Privacy Policy for Emberdice

Effective: November 25, 2026

Emberdice ("the game") is an Android game by Ash & Crown, a DBA of Marquez Enterprises LLC, in Lenoir, North Carolina, USA. The package name is com.ashandcrown.emberdice. This policy explains what data the game handles.

Data stored on your device

All game progress is stored only on your device. That includes your save, unlocks, currency balances, and purchase ledger. There is no account, and the game does not use any servers of ours.

Personal data

We do not collect, store, or share personal data. The game has no analytics, no advertising SDK, and no crash reporting SDK.

If we add an opt-in rewarded ad feature later, we will update this policy first.

In-app purchases

In-app purchases include non-consumable unlocks and cosmetics, plus consumable keys, embers, and chest bundles. Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles payment information under Google's privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details.

We receive only the purchase tokens needed to grant the items you buy. Those tokens are stored on your device.

Randomized chests

Drop odds for randomized chests are shown in the game before you open or buy one. Chest contents are cosmetics, soft currency, or unlock shards. You cannot cash them out, and they have no real-world value.

Permissions the game requests

  • INTERNET: so Google Play Billing can complete purchases.
  • VIBRATE: for haptic feedback.
  • BILLING: so Google Play can process in-app purchases.

Children's privacy

Emberdice is not directed at children under 13. It is rated for general audiences.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game, or clearing the app's data, removes everything the game has stored. There is no copy on our servers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game evolves. Continued use of the game after changes means you accept the updated policy.
